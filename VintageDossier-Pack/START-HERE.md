# START HERE: Vintage Dossier documentary video pack
Dark archival desk, typewriter labels, red pen and strings, living maps with glowing countries and cartoon war icons,
and a camera that glides through one continuous space. True-story niche: crime, incidents, British Raj, 70s-90s, how wars started.

## How to use (no powerful PC needed: everything runs in Claude's cloud environment)
1. Open a **new Claude Code chat** and attach this zip.
2. Write: **"MASTER-PROMPT.md padho aur kaam shuru karo."** (or the same in English)
3. Claude installs everything itself (about 2-4 min), says **Ready**, then goes step by step.
   **Each step waits for you** (reply "next" or ask for changes):
   1. **Topics**: 10 ideas, you pick a number (or give your own topic)
   2. **Research**: facts, timeline, sources
   3. **Length**: choose 2 to 20 minutes
   4. **Script**: full narration + how to record the voice
   5. **Asset list**: what Claude makes itself, what you download (exact search terms), what you generate with AI (copy-paste prompts)
   6. **Send material**: voiceover + images (tip: put all images in ONE zip). Claude ticks off what arrived.
   7. Write **start**: Claude builds the whole video and gives you the final MP4
   8. **Changes**: tell the time (mm:ss) and what to change
4. Add your music in any editor using the trimmed voiceover Claude gives you.

## Good to know
- Render time is about 5 × the video length (a 10-min video ≈ 50 min). Claude tells you the estimate at Stage 3.
- The cloud environment is temporary: **download the MP4 as soon as you get it.**
- Use real archive photos (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress, national archives) at 1000 px or more for sharp prints.
- Claude cannot hear audio: listen to the SFX levels yourself and ask for louder/softer if needed.
- A new topic? Start a new chat with the zip (Claude remembers past topics through `02-videos/_ledger.md` if you keep the updated pack).
